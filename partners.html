Who we
work with

Most of our partners are U.S. small businesses with 5 to 50 people, where one strong office hire changes everything. Our team members are based in the Philippines, Mexico, Nigeria, and other countries across Latin America, Africa, and Asia, work your business hours, and join as full-time members of your team.

Home services

Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, landscaping. The phones never stop, the techs are in the field, and the office is where jobs get booked, dispatched, and billed. Hybryd team members take the dispatch board, customer calls, scheduling, and invoicing follow-up so your techs stay on jobs and your owner stops answering the phone at dinner.

Pest control

Seasonal spikes, recurring service routes, and a lot of inbound calls. We place customer support reps, route managers, and office coordinators who handle scheduling, renewals, and payment follow-up. Ecoguard Pest Control has been working with Hybryd team members on exactly this.

Roofing and construction

Estimates, permits, supplier orders, subcontractor paperwork, and customer updates between crews. A Hybryd operations support team member or AR/AP clerk keeps the office side moving while your project managers stay on site. The Roofing Company and Bell Mountain Builders are two of our partners here.

Dental offices

Front desk overflow, insurance verification, recall scheduling, and patient follow-up. Remote dental admins work your office hours, answer in English, and clear the backlog that in-office staff never get to between patients. If you run a dental practice, our sister company Denterra focuses on this full time at hiredenterra.com.

Software and SaaS

Customer support, onboarding, QA, and the internal ops tasks that pile up as you grow. Rentkit has relied on Hybryd for customer support and training talent for over a year. We find people who can learn your product and talk to your customers like they work there, because they do.

Professional services

Law, accounting, insurance, consulting. Client intake, document prep, calendar management, billing, and the admin work that pulls billable people off billable work. A full-time remote office manager or admin from Hybryd costs a fraction of a local hire and sticks around.

Real estate

Transaction coordination, listing admin, lead follow-up, and showing scheduling. We place coordinators and admins who keep every file moving and every lead answered the same day, so agents spend their time with clients instead of paperwork.

Cleaning companies

Quotes, recurring schedules, crew coordination, and customer check-ins. A Hybryd team member manages the calendar, confirms jobs, handles reschedules, and follows up on reviews and payments, so the owner can focus on crews and growth.
